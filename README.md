## 🔢 Part 1: Math Problems

This section evaluates four numbers based on multiple conditions.

### ✅ Checks Performed:
- Do the numbers add up to 50?
- Are at least two numbers odd?
- Are all numbers 25 or less?
- Are all numbers unique?
- Are all numbers divisible by 5?
- Is the first number greater than the last?

### ➗ Arithmetic Chain:
- Subtract first number from second  
- Multiply result by third number  
- Find remainder when divided by fourth number  

### ✔ Final Validation:

All conditions are combined into a single boolean result (`isValid`).

## 🚗 Part 2: Practical Math – Road Trip

### 📍 Scenario:

* Total distance: **1500 miles**
* Fuel budget: **$175**
* Fuel cost: **$3/gallon**

### 🚘 Speed Comparisons:

| Speed  | MPG    | Goal                 |
| ------ | ------ | -------------------- |
| 55 MPH | 30 MPG | Best fuel efficiency |
| 60 MPH | 28 MPG | Balanced             |
| 75 MPH | 23 MPG | Fastest              |

### 📊 Calculations:

For each speed:

* Fuel needed (gallons)
* Total fuel cost
* Budget check
* Travel time (hours)

### 🏁 Conclusion:

* **55 MPH** → Cheapest but slowest
* **75 MPH** → Fastest but most expensive
* **60 MPH** → Best balance between time and cost ✅
